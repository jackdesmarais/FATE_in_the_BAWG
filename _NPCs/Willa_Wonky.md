# Willa Wonky

The owner of the [Wonky Factory](/FATE_in_the_BAWG/locations/Wonky_factory.html), a chocolate factory in [Coco Cove](/FATE_in_the_BAWG/locations/Coco_Cove.html). A flamboyant and eccentric candy maker. Used to hold factory tours until a disasterous tour lead to a series of child deaths and deeply traumatized [Taffy](/FATE_in_the_BAWG/PCs/taffy_tawffee.html)
