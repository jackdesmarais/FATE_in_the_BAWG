# Sintered Crystal

A material made from compacted glitter from the [drifts](/FATE_in_the_BAWG/locations/The_Drifts.html) fused into solid crystal with intense light. A major export of [Aeol](/FATE_in_the_BAWG/locations/Aeol.html) and the primary construction material used to build the city.
