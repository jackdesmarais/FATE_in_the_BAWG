# The Hall of the Roof Wardens

A massive complex of tunnels in [The Depths](/FATE_in_the_BAWG/locations/The_Depths.html), that is used as the primary base of the [Roof Wardens](/FATE_in_the_BAWG/factions/Roof_Wardens.html). Contains [Jeref Owne's](/FATE_in_the_BAWG/NPCs/Jeref_Owne.html) office.
